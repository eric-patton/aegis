## Prompt set

All boards used the built-in image-generation path and the approved Aegis mockups as
strict visual-language references.

- Theme parity: render identical world and Field Drawer geometry in complete light and
  dark themes, with equal hierarchy, states, semantic colors, and contrast.
- Responsive geometry: compare wide desktop, the supported 1100 by 700 minimum,
  150 percent Character Creation, and 200 percent vertically stacked Conversation.
- Interaction states: compare focus and selection, unmet requirements, reversible
  zero-cost action, irreversible confirmation, target cycling, and follow-tail.
- Destinations and boundaries: compare light and dark full-window tasks, narrow campaign
  entry, and shared saving, destructive confirmation, and load-failure states.

Generated names, values, icons, maps, and prose are illustrative only. The boards do not
alter engine, content, save, replay, or deterministic behavior.
